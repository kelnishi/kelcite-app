# Changelog 0.9.2

Released 2026-10-12.

**Skeletonize** turns a mesh or clay body into a printable strut lattice, and **Bake** sets resolution, triangle count, smoothing and snapping with a live preview.

## Modifiers

Wiki: [Modifiers](Tool-Modifiers)

- Truss or Voronoi linking, through the volume, over the surface, or both, with separate pieces bridged through the body

### Skeletonize

- Turns a mesh or clay body into a printable strut lattice, previewed live and baked to a mesh; the Skeleton tool (L) picks the bodies

## Bake

Wiki: [Bake an SDF to a Mesh](Workflow-Bake-an-SDF-to-a-Mesh)

- Bake sets resolution, triangle count, smoothing and snapping, with a live preview
