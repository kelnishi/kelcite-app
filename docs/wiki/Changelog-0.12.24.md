# Changelog Feature Release 0.12.24

Released 2026-10-19.

**Refine with vision** in the auto-rig looks at nine views of the figure to place its cuts, and a Rotatrix trackball works through SatMouse.

## Rig and Pose

Wiki: [Rig and Pose a Character](Workflow-Rig-and-Pose-a-Character)

- Refine with vision, in the generic auto-rig's Limbs step, looks at nine views of the figure and moves the faces near each cut to the limb the views agree on
- The generic auto-rig finds an arm whose hand rests on the hip and a figure whose feet are joined by a base, starts a thick upper arm at the shoulder, and starts both legs of a flat-footed figure at the same height

## Objects and the interface

- A Rotatrix 3D trackball works through SatMouse 0.19, as a SpaceMouse does
- The Asset Library no longer lists Quaternius, whose files a browser can't download
