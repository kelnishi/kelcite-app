# Changelog 0.2.3

Released 2026-02-28.

Split separates its result into pieces and **previews its cut loops**, and Move snaps to surfaces and pins.

## Split and Combine

Wiki: [Split and Cut a Model](Workflow-Split-and-Cut-a-Model)

- Split separates its result into pieces, previews its cut loops, and heals any loop you deselect
- Combine carries pins from both meshes to the result

## Move

Wiki: [Construction and Rigging](Tool-Construction-and-Rigging#move)

- Move snaps to surfaces with Shift and to pins with Alt

## Objects and the interface

- Focusing the same selection again steps the view back, and the camera is kept between sessions
