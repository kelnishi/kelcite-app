# Changelog Feature Release 0.12.7

Released 2026-10-17.

Downloads lists each optional download's **licenses**, and a download with different terms waits until you accept them.

## Files and the tour

Wiki: [Getting Started](Workflow-Getting-Started#the-demo-tour)

- Downloads lists each optional download's licenses, with their licensors and links to their texts
- A download whose terms differ from Kelcite's own waits until you accept them
- Files that Kelcite may not host download from their publisher, checked against the version Kelcite was tested with, and Downloads says when the publisher no longer offers them
