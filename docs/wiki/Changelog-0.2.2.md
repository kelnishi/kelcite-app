# Changelog 0.2.2

Released 2026-02-27.

Split places a pin at each cut loop, **Part** groups the separated pieces, and an **About and FAQ** panel arrives.

## Split and Combine

Wiki: [Split and Cut a Model](Workflow-Split-and-Cut-a-Model)

- Split places a pin at each cut loop, facing out of each piece
- Part puts separated pieces in a named group and keeps their pins

## Pin and Wire

Wiki: [Construction and Rigging](Tool-Construction-and-Rigging)

- Pins hide with their mesh, can sit anywhere on a face, and snap to vertices with Shift

## Repair

Wiki: [Repair a Scan for Printing](Workflow-Repair-a-Scan-for-Printing)

- Repair fixes flipped normals, and an About and FAQ panel is added
