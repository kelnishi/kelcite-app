# Changelog 0.12.0

Released 2026-10-16.

**Downloads** adds optional components such as STEP import, the Pose tool maps rigs to a **humanoid** with presets, and rigs export to **FBX** for Unity, Blender and Daz Studio.

## Rig and Pose

Wiki: [Rig and Pose a Character](Workflow-Rig-and-Pose-a-Character)

- The Pose tool's Humanoid section maps a rig's bones to a humanoid's, and presets pose a mapped figure on its own proportions: Victory, Fist pump, Arms up, Relaxed stand and T-pose
- Export to FBX for Unity, Blender and Daz Studio, with a template that names the bones and sets the file's conventions

## Files and the tour

Wiki: [Getting Started](Workflow-Getting-Started#the-demo-tour)

- Downloads lists optional components, such as STEP import, that you choose to download and that then work offline; STEP import no longer downloads by itself
- Saved glTF files open at their real size in other apps, and a saved scene keeps a rig's humanoid mapping
