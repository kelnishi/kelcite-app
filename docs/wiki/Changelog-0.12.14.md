The Rig tool works in **three stages**: Skeleton, Weights and Test.

## Rig and Pose

Wiki: [Rig and Pose a Character](Workflow-Rig-and-Pose-a-Character)

- The Rig tool works in three stages, Skeleton, Weights and Test; the humanoid auto-rig, the generic auto-rig and placing by hand are the Skeleton stage's three ways to make bones
- By hand, the Rig tool lists the rig as chains, and the chain or joints you pick get the edits that apply to them, such as Re-fit, Add chain, Add fingers and twist bones
- The Rig tool's explanations moved into its controls' tooltips
