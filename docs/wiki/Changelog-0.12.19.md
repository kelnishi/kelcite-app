# Changelog Feature Release 0.12.19

Released 2026-10-19.

The generic auto-rig pairs symmetric figures correctly and reads limbs more reliably.

## Rig and Pose

Wiki: [Rig and Pose a Character](Workflow-Rig-and-Pose-a-Character)

- The generic auto-rig pairs a figure that is symmetric top to bottom by left and right, instead of pairing its top limbs with its bottom ones
- A limb ending in a flat face ends its chain on the limb's axis, and Core S starts a thick arm at the shoulder instead of the elbow
- Core G no longer reads a flat-topped body's rim as a limb
