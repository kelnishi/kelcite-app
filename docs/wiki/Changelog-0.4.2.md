# Changelog Feature Release 0.4.2

Released 2026-03-09.

Kelcite is available in **13 languages**, and the **Wire** tool connects pins with straight or curved wires.

## Pin and Wire

Wiki: [Construction and Rigging](Tool-Construction-and-Rigging)

- The Wire tool connects pins with straight or curved wires, with tangent handles and distance labels
- Pin's loop mode finds the loops around a face group and snaps to flat faces on clay; Inclination sets how sharp a crease counts, and Alt+A pins every free face
